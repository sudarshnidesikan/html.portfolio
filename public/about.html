About Me

I'm a Senior UX/UI Designer at TAO Digital Solutions, passionate about creating intuitive, meaningful and technology-driven digital experiences.

My work focuses on transforming complex ideas and user needs into simple, effective and engaging product experiences. I enjoy working across the end-to-end design process, from understanding user problems and defining information architecture to wireframing, prototyping and crafting polished user interfaces.

Beyond traditional UX/UI design, I have experience designing AI-powered applications and workflows, focusing on how users interact with intelligent systems. This includes translating AI capabilities into intuitive user experiences, designing AI-driven interactions and making complex AI functionalities accessible and easy to use.

My areas of expertise include:

  • UX Strategy & User-Centred Design
  • UI Design & Interaction Design
  • AI-Powered Product Experiences
  • AI Interaction & Workflow Design
  • Product Design & Information Architecture
  • Wireframing & Interactive Prototyping
  • Design Systems & Responsive Design
  • Web & Mobile Application Design

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of UX, product thinking and artificial intelligence, exploring how thoughtful design can make emerging technologies more useful, accessible and human-centred.

I believe the future of digital products lies not just in what technology can do, but in how naturally people can interact with it.
